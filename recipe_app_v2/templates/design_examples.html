Sticky Gochujang Chicken

Weeknight dinnersFavorites

Garlicky yogurt, a spoonful of chile paste, and a little sweetness make a fast marinade with beautifully charred edges.

Total
35 min
Prep
15 min
Cook
20 min
Serves
4
Original recipeSource recipe PDF

Cooked 3 times 12 recorded views In meal plan

Your note Double the sauce for rice bowls tomorrow.

Gather around

Ingredients (6)

4 servings

  • 1½ lb. boneless chicken thighs
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 3 Tbsp. gochujang
  • 3 cloves garlic, grated
  • 2 Tbsp. maple syrup
  • 2 Tbsp. soy sauce

A little char is a good thing

Instructions

  1. 01

    Whisk yogurt, gochujang, garlic, maple syrup, and soy sauce until smooth. Reserve a spoonful for serving.

  2. 02

    Turn chicken in the marinade until coated. Rest 20 minutes, or cover and chill for up to 8 hours.

  3. 03

    Broil on an oiled rack, turning once, until cooked through and burnished at the edges, 15–18 minutes. Rest briefly and serve.

Sunday Night Lasagna

Sunday suppersFamily favorites

Slow-simmered tomato sauce, creamy ricotta, and browned edges make this the kind of dinner that earns a permanent place in the rotation.

Total
1 hr 25 min
Prep
35 min
Cook
50 min
Serves
8
Original recipeView recipe on Whisk

Cooked once 8 recorded views Not planned

Your note Assemble in the morning and bake just before dinner.

Layer by layer

Ingredients (6)

8 servings

  • 12 sheets lasagna noodles
  • 3 cups tomato sauce
  • 15 oz. ricotta
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1 lb. ground beef
  • 1 large egg

Take your time with the layers

Instructions

  1. 01

    Brown the beef in a wide pan. Stir in tomato sauce and simmer while you prepare the noodles and ricotta mixture.

  2. 02

    Mix ricotta with egg. Layer sauce, noodles, ricotta, and mozzarella in a baking dish; repeat, finishing with sauce and cheese.

  3. 03

    Cover and bake until bubbling. Uncover for the last 15 minutes to brown the top. Rest 15 minutes before slicing.

Smashed Cucumber Salad

Lunch & sidesFavoritesNever cooked

Craggy cucumber pieces catch every drop of a garlicky, chile-laced dressing. Cold, crisp, and ready before the rice is done.

Total
15 min
Prep
15 min
Serves
4
Original recipe

Not cooked yet 2 recorded views Not planned

Your note Add the dressing just before serving to keep the crunch.

Cool and crunchy

Ingredients (6)

4 servings

  • 4 Persian cucumbers
  • 2 cloves garlic, grated
  • 1 red chile, thinly sliced
  • 2 Tbsp. rice vinegar
  • 1 Tbsp. toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tsp. sesame seeds

Five minutes to better cucumbers

Instructions

  1. 01

    Lightly smash the cucumbers with the side of a knife, then tear into bite-size pieces. Salt and let drain for 5 minutes.

  2. 02

    Stir garlic, chile, vinegar, and sesame oil together. Pat cucumbers dry, then toss with the dressing.

  3. 03

    Finish with sesame seeds. Serve cold alongside rice or anything fresh from the grill.
