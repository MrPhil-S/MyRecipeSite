Gochujang Yogurt Chicken

A tangy, gently fiery yogurt marinade turns chicken thighs glossy and charred at the edges. Save a little sauce for the table.

30 minTotal time
4–6Servings
EasyDifficulty
Jump to recipe ↓

What you’ll need

Ingredients

Makes 4–6 servings

  • 4–6 garlic cloves
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • ½ cup gochujang
  • 3 Tbsp. maple syrup
  • 3 Tbsp. soy sauce
  • 2½–3 lb. chicken thighs

Let the heat do its thing

Method

  1. 01

    Whisk garlic, yogurt, gochujang, maple syrup, soy sauce, oil, salt, and pepper until smooth. Set aside a little sauce for serving, if you like.

  2. 02

    Coat the chicken in the marinade. Rest for 20 minutes at room temperature, or refrigerate for up to 12 hours.

  3. 03

    Broil on an oiled rack, turning once, until the chicken is cooked through and burnished at the edges, 15–18 minutes. Rest for 5 minutes.

Type & rhythm

A compact editorial serif leads; a crisp humanist sans keeps recipes easy to scan.

Cards align to a 12-column rhythm, with generous 28px gutters and a 4:3 food crop. Titles stay close to the image; cooking time and yield share one quiet line.

Principles

One restrained persimmon accent marks what matters. High-contrast ink carries the story. Food photography provides the color.

Favorite hearts sit directly on the image, in a clean white disc. No card chrome competes with the meal.
